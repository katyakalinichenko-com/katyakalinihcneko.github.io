I’m a Ukrainian-born (2000) graphic designer based in the Netherlands, currently studying at ArtEZ University of the Arts in Arnhem. My practice spans printed matter, photography, moving image, and other visual forms. Working through intuition, research, and experimentation, I explore unexpected associations and tensions between rational and irrational, familiar and strange. My work often recontextualises existing images, objects, and ideas to create new meanings, leaving room for ambiguity, interpretation, and irony.
