I’m a Ukrainian-born (2000) graphic designer based in the Netherlands, currently studying at ArtEZ University of the Arts in Arnhem. My work spans printed matter, photography, moving images, and other visual forms. Starting with questions and following my creative intuition, I use research and experimentation to discover unexpected connections between images, objects, ideas, and cultural references. I am especially interested in the tensions between personal and collective, rational and irrational, and familiar and strange. Rather than seeking clear-cut answers, I create situations that invite multiple interpretations, often through recontextualization, ambiguity and irony.
